Nashville Forum Constitution Template

Clear is kind and kind is clear.

This is the EO Nashville chapter's forum constitution template, sorted by topic so a forum can read it and fill it out in one sitting. Nothing was added and nothing was taken out. The fines are in one table, every vote is in one table, and the blanks to fill in are listed in Appendix B.
