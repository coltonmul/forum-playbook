What a facilitation is made of

Half one

The health of the forum

The connections between members, best practices, and new tools for deep dives, presentations and exercises. A life audit usually lands here, and the What's Next exercise.

Half two

The theme

An extended exercise, or a series of them, built from three inputs: the moderator's vision for the forum, what the forum says it wants, and the forum health survey. The common themes: purpose in meeting; purpose and meaning in life; new ways to share vulnerably, so the connections go deeper; goals and KPIs for yourself and your business; vision and planning, so people live more intentionally.

Popular retreats

Wed / 2026-10-28 / 9:35 AM CT · the themes forums pick most

Most forums build the theme half of their retreat around one of these six. Colton shapes each one around your forum, and builds a new one when none of these is right.

Forum 14, Telluride, August 2025

Under the stars

North Star

After dark, the forum goes outside with a green laser pointer. Each member names the North Star they inherited and the one that is truly theirs, personally and professionally, and the forum talks it through.

A snapshot of your life today

Life Wheel

Each member scores the main areas of their life, like health, family, friends, work and money, from 1 to 10. The shape of the wheel shows at a glance where life is full and where it is running thin, and gives the forum a place to start.

Living into your meaning

Ikigai

Four questions: what you love, what you are good at, what the world needs from you, and what you can be paid for. Built for entrepreneurs, it helps each member see where their time goes now and how to spend more of their life where the four meet.

Goals the forum can hold you to

Goal Setting and Accountability

Members set goals and KPIs for themselves and their business, say them out loud to the forum, and agree on how the forum checks in on them at the monthly meetings.

What made you who you are

Values and Meaning

Members look back at the people and moments that shaped their beliefs, their dreams and their insecurities, often by drawing the highs and lows of their life as a lifeline, then share that story with the forum.

How others see you

My Identity vs. My Values

Built on the Johari window. Before the retreat, each member asks the ten people closest to them how they see them. In the room, they set those answers beside how they see themselves and the values they say they hold, and the forum helps make sense of the gaps.
