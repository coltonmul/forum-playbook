A retreat exercise

North Star

A reflection on direction. Each member names the North Star they inherited, the one that is theirs now, and the stars they will steer by this year, then leaves with one page of intentions to take home. It works any time; under a night sky, Colton points out the real one with a green laser pointer.

What it is for

Mon / 2026-11-16 / 8:10 AM CT · direction, not specifics

North Star is about which way each member is facing, so nobody needs a number that night. It pairs well with goal setting at a later retreat, which turns the direction into goals the forum can check.

The North Star you inherited

Most of us steer by one someone handed us: a parent, a faith, a family business, a hometown. Members name theirs, and which parts they still follow.

The one that is yours

One sentence, in their own words. Some point inward, at who they are becoming; some point outward, at the people they serve.

The stars around it

The North Star holds still while the sky turns around it. For the business, a spouse, the kids, health and friends: the one star to steer by this year.

One page to keep

Before heading home, each member turns the reflection and the conversation into a few intentional goals or decisions. It is the page they keep, and the one they look at a year later.

How it runs

Mon / 2026-11-16 / 8:10 AM CT · before, during and after

  1. Before. Reflect

    On your own · about an hour

    Most of the work happens ahead of time. Each member spends an hour with the worksheet: the North Star they inherited, the one that is theirs, and the stars around it.

  2. During. Share

    With the forum

    Each member shares with the forum. These are people who care about them and know them better than almost anyone else, so their perspectives are worth hearing (perspective, not advice).

  3. After. The page to keep

    Before heading home

    While still removed from the day-to-day, each member turns the reflection and the conversation into a few intentional goals or decisions: one page to keep and look at a year later.

A bonus under a night sky: Colton points out Polaris with a green laser pointer, and a stargazing app on any phone will find it.

What the forum takes home

Mon / 2026-11-16 / 8:10 AM CT · for each member, and for the moderator

  • A worksheet for each member: the North Star they inherited, the one that is theirs, and the stars around it, mostly done before the retreat.
  • The page to keep: each member's North Star, the stars they will steer by, and their intentions headed home.
  • A facilitator's guide for the moderator, so the forum can run North Star again on its own.
  • A check-in at six months and at the next retreat, against the page they kept.

Any time, anywhere. North Star works in a living room or on a mountain deck. It is a bonus under a night sky, and a stargazing app on any phone will point the forum to the North Star.

The worksheet and the guide come with a facilitated retreat; they are not posted on this site.
