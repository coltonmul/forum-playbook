Forum Health Day

Chapters name it what fits: Forum Health Day, Forum Health Summit, Connection Day, Advanced Forum Skills. Underneath, it runs as an EO Forum Next Level, which is how EO Global helps pay for it. I deliver what a Forum Next Level requires, then build the rest of the day around what your chapter actually needs.

I pitch the content as new tools. Most of it is the forum fundamentals every member should know and most have forgotten. Calling it new makes it exciting instead of making anyone feel behind.

Before the day

  • Six to eight weeks outA call with your chapter manager and forum chair: goals, what the board wants to move, and what to call the day.
  • Four to six weeks outYour forums take the Forum Health survey (EO's, or a short custom one). The results write the agenda. I compare them against years of chapter data.
  • Two weeks outPromo copy and a short promo video for your chapter to send, at no extra cost. Final run of show agreed.

The trip

  • The night beforeI fly in. Moderator dinner or happy hour if you want one: Q&A, catch up, whatever your moderators need.
  • The day: arriveDoors, lunch, and the chapter's own business if you want to pair it with an all-member meeting.
  • The day: the sessionThe tools, built from your survey, closing with live open coaching.
  • The day: Forum MashupEveryone splits into mixed groups of five or six, members who do not share a forum, with a volunteer moderator and a worksheet. I brief those moderators beforehand.
  • The day: wrapBack in one room for rapid-fire takeaways and commitments. A hard stop so parents can leave.
  • AfterHappy hour, where I stay for individual and forum-level questions. Fly home that night or the next morning.

Length is your call. Four hours works. One chapter ran eight. Lunch to happy hour, or breakfast to happy hour. Expect 50 to 75 percent of the chapter to show.

The moderator layer

Six months after forum training, members remember about half of it. A year later, about 20 percent. Moderators feel that drop first, and they carry the most weight. The moderator layer is less curriculum, more craft: behavior, leadership and the tools that make a meeting work.

What we cover

  • Finding the real 5%, so there is material worth a deep dive
  • Running a deep dive that goes somewhere
  • Using the parking lot instead of losing it
  • Open coaching, and when to step in or out
  • Setting a theme and goals for the moderator year, then feeding it forward to the next moderator
  • Whatever your moderators raise. I ask them before I build it.

Two formats

  • Moderator dinner, the night before the chapter day. Private, informal, Q&A.
  • Half-day moderator session, before or after the chapter day, when you want real practice time.
  • Pick the date by polling your moderators first, then open the chapter day to everyone.
  • My bias: anyone passionate about forum belongs in the room. They will be a moderator soon.

Themed office hours

Support between visits without paying for a trip. One hour, once a quarter, on a theme your forum chair picks. It runs as a conversation between me and the chair, then opens up for questions. The chapter records it and shares it, so the members who could not make it still get it.

Open drop-in hours sound good and draw nobody. A theme and a host give people a reason to show up.

Forum retreats

For one forum, at its own retreat. I take the front of it, two half days or one long day, then leave the forum to run with it. I fly in the night before.

Part 1: Forum optimization

I can also point you to retreat houses I have worked from, from Puerto Vallarta to Big Sky, and connect you with a retreat planner. A custom forum app and site are available as an add-on.

Ideas I hand over, free

Social placement for new members. New members should get offers from two forums, not a rejection from one after paying their dues. Run member onboarding with the chapter manager, president and membership chair, then a speed networking hour right after where forums looking for members meet every new member at once. Forums that want members send someone. It creates urgency and reverses the scarcity.

Launchpad ambassadors. If your new members start in Launchpad forums, let forums that are recruiting send one ambassador to sit in. Meeting socially plus a Launchpad together is the interview.

Pilots and tools. Regional Training Days, a membership intake and vetting tool, and more live on the Concepts page.

Questions chapters ask

Doesn't EO Global already have a product for this?

Yes: Forum Next Level, and this runs as one. The standard version is in-the-box curriculum built to cover a lot. Running it this way keeps the Global funding and lets the day address what your forums actually told us.

Can we make it moderators only?

You can, but Global's share needs at least 12 members in the room, and most chapters get more out of opening it up. The common setup: moderator dinner or session for moderators, the chapter day for everyone.

What if fewer than 12 people come?

Then Global's share is at risk and the chapter would cover more of it. Pick the date with your moderators first, put it on the chapter calendar early, and use the promo video. Most chapters land well above 12.

We already have a forum speaker booked this year. Does that conflict?

Usually not. A speaker on one topic and a day of forum tools stack well. Spacing them a few months apart helps both.

Can we add our FTP or MTP to the same trip?

Yes, and it is the cheapest way to do it. One flight, an extra hotel night, and the chapter gets two or three things done.
