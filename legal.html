Forum Playbook privacy policy and terms of use

01
Privacy Policy

Forum Playbook is run by Colton Mulligan in Nashville, Tennessee, USA. For questions, or to see, correct or delete what this site holds about you, email colton@foxfuelcreative.com.

Every page of this site counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It records page views, the referring site, your country and your device type. It sets no cookies and does not fingerprint your browser. The reason is to know which resources people use (a legitimate interest). Cloudflare keeps this data under Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

If you sign up for the app beta at forumplaybook.com/app-beta, we keep what you type into that form: your name, the email address tied to your Apple ID, your chapter and forum, the features you picked, any forum mates' email addresses you add, and anything you write in the last box. It is stored in a Cloudflare database and seen only by Colton, who is building the app. It is used to invite you, and anyone you list, to the TestFlight beta, to set up your forum's profile, and to decide what to build first. Sending a TestFlight invite gives Apple the email address, under Apple's Privacy Policy. It is not sold or shared for marketing. It is kept until the beta ends or you ask for it to be deleted. If you add forum mates' email addresses, let them know you did.

The Catalyst Collective pages (everything under forumplaybook.com/concepts/catalystcollective) have a feedback box, surveys and a fit finder. Each one sends your name, email and message (or your answers) to a Cloudflare database at catalystcollective.pages.dev. Your name and message appear on the page for other readers; your email does not. The reason is your consent when you submit. It is kept until the project ends or you ask for it to be deleted.

Each forum has its own site at <forum>.forumplaybook.com. A forum's members sign in there with their name, the email on file and the forum's passphrase, and some forums answer a retreat survey there. Each forum site has its own legal page that lists what it keeps.

Some services are contacted by your browser directly. The resource library is read from Google Drive, and its files open on drive.google.com. Videos and the video list come from YouTube. The "Download on the App Store" badge comes from Apple. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Each of these receives your IP address, under its own privacy practices.

You can ask what this site holds about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, at the email above. If you are in the EU, the UK or Switzerland, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

02
The iOS Apps

This section covers the Forum Playbook iOS apps: Forum Timer and Forum Playbook.

The Forum Timer and Forum Playbook apps have no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Rosters, agendas, timers, favorites and question history you enter are kept on your device. If you load your forum with its code and passphrase, the app downloads your forum's roster and constitution from your forum's site.

Forum Timer plays audio alerts even when your phone is on silent. That's the entire point of the app, not a data practice, but it's worth saying plainly: the app uses your speaker, not your microphone.

03
Terms of Use

The resources on this site -- templates, frameworks, guides, and worksheets -- are here to be used. That's the whole point. Take them, adapt them, run better Forums with them.

A few things to keep in mind: where a resource credits a source, respect that credit. Some materials were created by Colton Mulligan, some were adapted from others' work, and some are widely circulated with unclear origins. Sources are cited when known. If something is yours and it's not credited correctly, reach out and it'll get fixed.

Don't repackage these resources and sell them. Don't claim you made them if you didn't. Beyond that, use your judgment -- these are tools for facilitators, not legal landmines.

This site is provided as-is, with no guarantees. Forum facilitation is a human endeavor and no template is a substitute for actually showing up prepared. If something on this site is broken or wrong, it's probably Colton's fault. He doesn't know how to code.
