Inside the app

Your forum

Members, who moderates, who presents next, and when you meet. The next meeting sits on the home screen.

The parking lot

Every parked topic, EQ and IQ, longest waiting first. Pull one at random when the room needs it.

Size a topic

Four questions, then the format, the protocol in named steps, and a button that starts exactly that segment.

What it does

  • {{icon:timer}}The meeting, block by blockThe Standard Forum as a real four-block meeting: whose turn it is in type you can read across the table, and the rest rebalances when you add a minute.
  • {{icon:users-three}}Your forumThe roster, the rotation, the next meeting, and reminders that count back from it.
  • {{icon:list-checks}}Decisions and the debriefHousekeeping captured while everyone is still in the room, then the recap email in one tap.
  • {{icon:notepad}}Your 5% reflectionsWrite them, or photograph the sheet you filled in by hand. Private, on your phone.
  • {{icon:book-open}}The field guideFoundations, facilitation moves, open coaching, retreats and cheat sheets, searchable offline.
  • {{icon:chart-bar}}HistoryWhat every meeting actually took, block by block, and where your forum keeps losing time.
