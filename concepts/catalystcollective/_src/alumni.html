The alumni survey, a draft

Would the Collective represent real value to the people it is for?

EO Nashville is exploring how to expand the Catalyst experience beyond graduation. This is a draft survey for everyone who came through Catalyst: current Bridge members, lapsed Bridge members, and graduates who never joined Bridge. It asks where they are today, what Catalyst and Bridge did and did not do for them, and whether the Catalyst Collective, an ongoing membership by invitation, would represent real value to them, at what price. Twelve questions. It is not live and nothing has been sent.

{{icon:hourglass}}Draft, not live

No alumnus has seen this and nothing goes out until EO Nashville says so (Sameera's call on September 9, still standing). The page exists so the people shaping the program can read every question in one place, test the form, and cut or change what they want before anything is sent.

Edits are welcome: use the box at the bottom of this page, or reply on the email thread. Changes Sameera asks for on this survey ship on Colton's standing approval (November 2, 2026).

{{sh:users-three|Who it is for and how it would go out|The plan around the questions}}
  • {{icon:graduation-cap}}Audience. Every past Catalyst graduate: current Bridge members, lapsed Bridge members (Justin Plough by name), graduates who never joined Bridge, and the class in Catalyst now. One survey, with the first question sorting people into those groups.
  • {{icon:video-camera}}Format. A roughly 60-second explainer from Matt, Sameera or Colton, watched right before the survey so the concept is fresh, or the overview page read first. The video script is in the library. Then the twelve questions below, about six minutes.
  • {{icon:users-four}}Who sends it, and to whom first, is EO Nashville's call. The alumni list lives with Sameera. A first pass to the 5 or 6 focus-group candidates, then the wider list, was the September 8 suggestion.
  • {{icon:chats}}Follow-up. The next-to-last question asks who would join a small focus group with Sameera and Matt, so the survey recruits its own second round.
  • {{icon:tag}}The price question and the offer. The price question offers $2,500, $3,000, $3,500 and $4,000 and nothing lower: dues are paid up front for the year (Colton, November 3). Under the last question, the survey makes an offer: leave your name and email with an honest answer and your first year in the Collective is at the $2,500 price. Whether the price question stays in at all is EO Nashville's call; the survey works without it.
  • {{icon:flask}}Test freely. Everything submitted through the form on this page before the survey goes live is marked test and ignored in any tally. Answers are private; only the count is public.
{{sh:target|What the answers settle|Five decisions, then commentary}}
  1. {{icon:scales}}Does the Collective represent real value to alumni, in their own words, before anyone builds it?
  2. {{icon:git-branch}}Are strivers and stabilizers different customers, or the same customer at different moments? (The two-track forum question.)
  3. {{icon:list-checks}}Which programming is worth building first, by stated demand and price tolerance?
  4. {{icon:users-three}}Same-stage rooms or mixed rooms?
  5. {{icon:question}}Is the "it became redundant" complaint a format problem or a fit problem? Everything else is commentary.
