The alumni survey, a draft

Would the Collective represent real value to the people it is for?

EO Nashville is exploring how to expand the Catalyst experience beyond graduation. Twelve questions for everyone who came through Catalyst, about six minutes. A draft. It is not live and nothing has been sent.

{{sh:list-numbers|The point of the survey|Three things it asks, in order}}
  1. {{icon:map-pin}}Where you are today (questions 1 to 3). Which group you are in, what your week looks like, and whether growing past $1M is the goal. This sorts the answers by group and by stage.
  2. {{icon:graduation-cap}}What Catalyst and Bridge did for you (4 to 6). What you hoped for, which parts were worth it, and where the gap was.
  3. {{icon:users-three}}The Collective, and the price (7 to 12). After the 60-second explainer or the overview: is it real value, which room, what you would use, what you would pay, and whether you would go deeper in a small group. The offer sits under the last question.
