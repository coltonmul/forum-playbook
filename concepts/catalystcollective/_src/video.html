The 60-second video · internal · production script, draft 1

Three rooms, sixty seconds.

A prospect-facing spot that sells belonging at every stage, not thresholds. Beat by beat: voiceover, visuals, on-screen text, production notes. Written fresh on September 10 as part of the Three Tracks Kit, now kept here; mark it up and it gets cut to match. The plan is to show it right before the alumni survey, so the concept is fresh.
