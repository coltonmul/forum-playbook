{{sh:image|The logo files|SVG and PNG, light and dark}}

The lockup is always stacked: CATALYST on top, a rule the full width, COLLECTIVE beneath it. CATALYST is the wordmark from eonashville.com, traced to vector so it stays sharp at any size. COLLECTIVE is Roboto Black, EO's typeface. The colors are EO's own: base navy for CATALYST, EO Blue for the rule and COLLECTIVE, and white with a light blue on dark backgrounds. SVG for anything that scales, PNG (2400 pixels wide, transparent) for decks and documents. If the name changes, these change.
