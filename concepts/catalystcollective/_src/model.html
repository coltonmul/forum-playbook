How the model works, in plain words

  • Each year a Catalyst class graduates. The share who accept an invitation join the Collective that year (year 1 also starts with the recruited past graduates).
  • Everyone who is a member during a year pays that year's dues. Movement happens at year end.
  • From each new class, a fixed number graduate into EO within their first year. Of the continuing members, a share graduates into EO each year, and of everyone left, the retention share stays. The rest leave.
  • Costs are the dedicated resource, the fixed lines, the EC bundle per member if any, and the events: attendance times food and beverage, plus speaker, AV and venue, with the EO House share of events costing no rent. Contingency sits on top of all of it.
  • Break-even members is the fixed cost divided by the margin per member (dues minus the per-member EC cost). It ignores events, which mostly scale with attendance, so treat it as a floor.
  • The EO impact line is informational: EO dues and initiation are not Collective revenue, but they show what the pipeline is worth to the chapter.

Things the model cannot know yet

  • Whether Collective dues credit toward EO initiation (a chapter revenue question, not a Collective one).
  • What the EC bundle actually costs, and whether it is inside the dues or on top.
  • Whether repeat-Catalyst pricing changes Catalyst's own loss.
  • Belmont's workshop partnership: free, sponsored, or paid.
