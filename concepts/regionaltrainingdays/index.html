Regional Training Days

Their first EO trip could be their forum training. Intake classes are shrinking, so instead of a thin small-group FTP, a virtual session, or a wait for a class of six, pool new members from several chapters into one full training day, hosted by a different city each quarter.

EO US East, 21 chapters. Sample host cities in blue. Any chapter, or a trainer's home city, can host.
21chapters in US East
4training days a year, one per quarter
1full room each time, for all of us
95%*member retention

Picture it

Picture your newest member a month after joining. Instead of a three-person training in a conference room at home, they fly to another chapter's city. That night they are at dinner with new members from five chapters, and the trainer is at the table. The next day they train in a private room at one of the city's best restaurants, and they are on a flight home by early evening.

How it runs

One chapter hosts near the end of each quarter, or members fly to a trainer's home city and the trainer's travel cost goes away.

  1. Thursday afternoonMembers fly in from their chapters.
  2. Thursday nightHappy hour and dinner with the trainer, so they know the room before they lead it. The host chapter decides who else comes: board members, a welcome committee, or everyone. Gregarious members love meeting EO'ers from out of town.
  3. FridayTraining day: a full day in a private room, wrapped by late afternoon.
  4. Friday eveningFly home, or stay and make a weekend of it.

Why it works

For new members

  • Their chapter flew them out to their training, and they see how big EO really is.
  • They make friends in other chapters who keep showing up at regional events.
  • Friday trainings turn into weekend trips.

For chapters

  • A full training room. No $250-per-person fees for empty seats.
  • Trainer travel split several ways, or gone.
  • Less fighting over preferred trainers' dates, and fewer trainers capped out before July 1.
  • Better retention: forum members who visit outside their chapter renew at 95%.*

TrainerTravel split across chapters, or zero when the training is in the trainer's home city.
