What Never Changes

  • The flow of your current sheet, kept: date, then Career, Family, Self and Forum rated 1-10, then Headline, Significance / Impact, Emotions / Feelings (3+ words), What do I realize about myself?, then next month's personal wager and the cash on the line.
  • The column widths match your current sheet, so writing on it feels the same. Half-inch side margins keep the writing area 7.5 inches wide.
  • What the IQ zone costs: your current sheet gives each topic about 2.6 inches of height. Iteration A gives 2.19. Nearly all of that difference is the IQ zone itself.
  • EQ wears Burn, IQ wears Teal, in both versions. Color here only ever means "which kind of topic is this."
  • The neutral version has no mark, no name and no URL on the page, and none in the PDF's title or file name either. Same layout, same color.
  • One page, US Letter, fonts embedded, ready for GoodNotes or a printer. Every PDF here was checked to be exactly one page.
Yours to call

Three Questions

D1

Which iteration becomes the real v1.0 sheet?

What this is: all three are the same sheet. They differ only in how much the page explains itself, which trades against writing room (the numbers on each card). Mixing is fine.

Say: "go with A", "go with B", "go with C", or a mix like "B plus the check boxes from C"
D2

Should EQ stay orange and IQ stay teal?

What this is: your two EQ vs IQ one-pagers show EQ in Teal and IQ in Brick red, but only because the DO / DON'T card block comes in those two colors. Here EQ gets the brand's lead color because it is the lead zone, and IQ gets Teal so it never reads as the DON'T color now that it has its own place in the agenda.

Say: "keep the colors" or "match the one-pagers"
D3

Does the neutral version keep its footer credit?

What this is: the neutral footer reads "From EO Nashville's Forum 14." on the left and "Built by Colton with Claude" on the right. Your 2024 White Space form carried the Forum 14 line, and your built-by rule puts the credit on anything that reaches other people. If neutral should mean no names at all, it comes off.

Say: "keep the credit" or "strip the credit"
