THREE WAYS TO
FIX THE HOME­PAGE.

All three keep what you kept in July (the full-size headline, the three big black numbers, icon cards, one hub) and all three fix the same problems: type up to a 17px body, icons on everything, hover feedback on every item, the new light sweep that pulls your eye toward the buttons, and click-to-preview so people see the document before they download it. They differ on ONE thing: how someone who needs something right now finds it.

Pick one

The bet: nothing hidden. Every category sits open on the page as a grid of document cards; a click expands the card into a preview with big buttons. Your "more card style elements" hypothesis.
  • Watch a card expand in place with the real first page of the doc
  • Hover any card: the sweep plus lift makes clickability obvious
  • Role Kit chips ride under the hero as the optional side path

Either way

Universal, Whatever You Pick

  • Type scale up, site-wide: 17px body, 14px rows, 11px floor (today's homepage runs 8-11px). This is the July audit's fix, finally applied.
  • Hover on every interactive item: the locked two-wave stays on buttons; rows and cards get lift plus shadow so "this is clickable" is never a mystery.
  • The sheen sweep (new): a soft light band passes left to right on hover and exits where the buttons live. Needs your yes, then it gets written into the brand page motion spec.
  • Preview before download: real first-page screenshots straight from Drive's thumbnail service, automatic for every synced file, no manual screenshots ever. Folders and blocked files fall back to an icon.
  • Icons and buckets: every file type and category carries a line icon; contrast fixes ride along (no more banned grays as text).
  • Rollout after your pick: the winning layout becomes the real homepage; the interaction layer (type, hovers, sheen, previews) then rolls to /brand, /legal, and the one-pager listing too.

Honest Fine Print

These are direction mockups, not finished builds: download buttons hit the real Drive files and previews are the real documents, but Role Kit pages are stubs and the mockup data is a baked snapshot (the real homepage keeps auto-syncing from Drive per brand rule 03). July's rejected v2 layouts were not reused. The live homepage stays untouched until you pick.
