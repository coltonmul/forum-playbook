Alerts & Sound
Overtime sound
Tap ▶ to listen, then tap a row to select. The melody loops when time runs out: it enters clearly, pulls back so the speaker can wrap up, then builds. All melodies are public-domain compositions synthesized on the spot.
Instrument
Duet: add a low harmony
The voice every melody plays in. Guitar is a plucked string, Piano is warm, Chime is a pure tone. Duet mixes a second instrument playing low notes underneath. Richer, but audition it with ▶ first.
Time's up intensity
Shapes everything at zero: the on-screen wording, how bright and how fast the red steps up, and how quickly the music builds. Gentle eases in and tops out quieter; Aggressive hits hard and maxes out in about 12 seconds.
Pre-ending alert
The short heads-up beep BEFORE time runs out (not the overtime melody above). Also fires a brief screen flash. Vibration works where the browser supports it (most Android phones; iPhones ignore it).

The Forum Timer is the meeting timer from Forum Playbook. Tap a time, set it in the middle of the table, and let the color do the talking: green means talk, gold means land the plane, brick means the music is coming.

WEB APP v1.0.0 · UPDATED MON / 2026-08-10 / 1:00 PM CT

The Quick Timer

Tap +2:00 and go. Add time mid-run, chain timers ("3 minutes, then 2"), and save the cadences you use every meeting. Type any duration on the microwave keypad, SET chip up top.

Bars or Clock Face

A full-screen fill that drains as time passes, or an analog dial with the flip points notched on the rim. Tap the display to switch. Both turn green, gold, then brick at the same thresholds.

Agenda Mode

Run a full forum agenda: one-word check-in, member updates around your roster, Deep Dive protocol phases. It holds at zero until the moderator moves on, then reports planned vs. actual.

Overtime With Manners

At zero the music enters clearly, pulls back so the speaker can wrap up, then builds until the room surrenders. Seven public-domain melodies, synthesized live in piano, guitar, or chime.

Nothing Leaves Your Browser

No accounts and no meeting data collected. Templates, roster, cadences, and settings save on this device, and that is the whole data story.

Built for the Table

The screen stays awake while a timer runs, the tab title carries the countdown, and the clock is drift-free. Turn the volume up and tap once to unlock sound; the browser insists.

Coming soon to the iOS App Store

This web app is the full timer, free, right here. The native iOS app is in the works for the things a browser is not allowed to do: alerts that play through the silent switch and Do Not Disturb, automatic full brightness, and torch signals on the back of the phone for the people sitting behind it.
